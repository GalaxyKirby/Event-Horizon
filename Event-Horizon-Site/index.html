Welcome To The Event Horizon

Hello traveler! Event Horizon is a competitive Splatoon 3 team that was formed in 2024, with the captain and co-captain having met in 2022. Our main focus as a team is an even split between being friends and improving at the game. Plenty of game nights mixed in with our practices!

Our gamplay is specialized in coordination, picking a lot of weapons with supernova sized hitboxes that combo with each other and deny space.
