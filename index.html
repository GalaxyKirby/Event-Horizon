Welcome To The Event Horizon

Event Horizon is a long running competitive Splatoon 3 team with an aim of pushing our skills into high level play and being an inspirational example of what a long-running team can be. The team was created in 2023, being a culmination of players from a variety of old low level teams. The name “Event Horizon” didn’t have too complex of backstory; funnily enough, it was decided off of a poll of cool sounding space related names that some of our space enthusiasts thought up.

The team specializes in what could best be described as an “AOE Dive”, taking fights with weapons that have large hitboxes while also being *just* fast enough to fend for themselves against the evil .52 Gals of the world. The strategy is a bit unorthodox; however, we have always believed in everyone on the team winning with what they enjoy the most. We're always looking for new oppurtunities to improve! We take every game, no matter the result, as a new learning experience.
